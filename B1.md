# B1 — The Glacier cost gotcha

What's the actual cost impact of an early deletion after archival? How would you redesign the
lifecycle so this stops quietly costing more than budgeted?

## Actual cost impact 
1.**80-day Early deletion Penalty :**  S3 glacier flexible retrival ( and Glacier Instant Retrival) enforces a **90-day minimum storage duration** (180 days for glacier deep archieve ). Because the photo was archieved on the Day 30  and deleted on Day 40 (only 10 Days in Glacier),  AWs charges a pro rated early deletion fee for the **remainig 80 Days** at the glacier storage rate.

2.**Transition & Metadata Overhead:** You still pay the upfront `PUT`/Lifecycle transition request fee(`$0.03` per 1,000 objects in us-east-1) plus **40KB of storage overhead per object** (32KB in glacier + 8KB in S3 Standard for metadata) for the full 90 days which for small listing photos can exceed the cost of the photo bytes themselves.

##Redesign the Lifecycle 
* **Delay Glacier Transition Past the Deletion Window:** Increase the transition rule from `30 Days` to **`90 or 120 days`** after upload / sale, ensuring post-sale buyer/seller deletion request occur while ojects are still in S3 standard (which has no minimum storage duration)

* **Use S3 Intelligent_tiering or keep small Objects in Standard:** For photos under 128KB, avoid Glacier entirely because the 40KB per object metadata overhead negated archival savings. For larger photos during days 30-120, use **S3 standard-IA** (30 days min) or **s3 Intelligent-Tiering** (no min duration, no retrivel / transition fees.)
